Senior Database Reliability Engineer

Job Type

Full-time

Location

Remote

Job Summary

Join our team as a Senior Database Reliability Engineer, where you’ll play a central role in architecting, optimizing, and ensuring the stability of mission-critical PostgreSQL environments. You will leverage your deep technical expertise to drive database reliability, high availability, and performance at scale within a cutting-edge, cloud-native infrastructure. This is an excellent opportunity for talented engineers who are passionate about building robust data systems and thrive in collaborative, fast-paced environments.

Key Responsibilities

• Design, implement, and manage highly available, resilient PostgreSQL database architectures using AWS RDS and Aurora PostgreSQL.

• Optimize database performance by analyzing query execution plans, tuning indices, and implementing advanced partitioning strategies.

• Ensure disaster recovery readiness through robust backup, replication, and failover processes.

• Diagnose and resolve slow queries, locks, deadlocks, and complex bottlenecks impacting large-scale databases.

• Automate routine maintenance tasks and promote operational excellence across all database environments.

• Collaborate cross-functionally to support new features, data migrations, and ongoing application scaling.

• Maintain comprehensive documentation and demonstrate excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Required Skills and Qualifications

• 5+ years of experience managing and optimizing PostgreSQL databases in production settings.

• Expert-level knowledge of PostgreSQL internals, query tuning, indexing, partitioning, and replication.

• Proven hands-on experience with AWS RDS and Aurora PostgreSQL, including backup, recovery, and high-availability design.

• Strong analytical abilities in troubleshooting slow queries, execution plans, locks, and deadlocks.

• Demonstrated experience maintaining large-scale, high-traffic databases with millions of rows.

• Deep care for clear, proactive written and verbal communication.

• Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a remote team.

Preferred Qualifications

• Experience supporting data platforms, analytics workloads, ETL processes, and operational databases.

• Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-native service environments.

• Exposure to supporting modern data architectures and advanced monitoring tools.
