Role description (from micro1)

Role Title: Aerospace CAD Expert

Role Type: Contractor

Location: Remote

micro1 is engaging Aerospace CAD Experts to contribute to a dynamic customer project centered on high-fidelity, multi-part aerospace assemblies. In this role, you'll apply your expertise to help train next-generation AI systems. Your work will shape how models learn, reason, and perform through high-quality, real-world input. No prior experience in AI is required — your domain knowledge is what matters. If you have a background in mechanical engineering, aerospace, or industrial design and thrive on technical precision, this is an exciting chance to impact the future of engineering design support tools.

Scope of Work

Author objective, multi-part questions about aerospace assemblies, focusing on non-guessable queries that require exact, technical answers.

Review native FreeCAD files to analyze and understand complex mechanical assemblies.

Develop several high-quality questions per assembly, ensuring coverage of key features, functions, and relationships between parts.

Deliver clear, well-researched answers to each question with reference to the assembly data.

Collaborate via writing and occasional verbal communication to clarify requirements and ensure technical accuracy.

Maintain a standards-driven approach, ensuring questions and answers are comprehensive, relevant, and program-agnostic.

Contribute to documentation and feedback processes to improve future question sets and project workflows.

Preferred Qualifications

Demonstrated expertise in aerospace, mechanical engineering, or industrial design with a strong foundation in CAD principles.

Hands-on experience with multi-part CAD assemblies, including ability to interpret complex design intent and technical drawings.

Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with an ability to convey technical concepts clearly and concisely.

Familiarity with FreeCAD or similar CAD platforms (FreeCAD exposure is a plus, not required).

Proven ability to author precise technical questions and answers, ideally in an educational or documentation context.

Strong attention to detail and commitment to providing high-quality, objective content.

Interest in advancing technology and supporting the development of intelligent design systems.
