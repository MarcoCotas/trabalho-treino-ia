Accounts Payable Analyst

Job Type: Full-time

Location

Remote

Job Summary

Join our team as an Accounts Payable Analyst, where your expertise in payroll, accounts payable, and payroll taxes will play a pivotal role in maintaining precise financial operations. We are seeking a detail-oriented professional with 2 to 4 years of high-volume AP experience, eager to contribute to a dynamic and collaborative remote environment. This is an excellent opportunity to further develop your career while making a measurable impact on our financial efficiency and accuracy.

Key Responsibilities

- Process a high volume of invoices and expense reports with accuracy and timeliness

- Reconcile accounts payable transactions and resolve discrepancies promptly

- Support payroll processing and ensure the correct application of payroll taxes

- Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to address payment inquiries

- Maintain and update vendor records, ensuring compliance and completeness

- Assist with month-end closing activities and financial reporting

- Continuously seek process improvements to streamline AP operations

Required Skills and Qualifications

- 2 to 4 years of hands-on experience in accounts payable, preferably in high-volume settings

- Strong knowledge of payroll functions and payroll tax regulations

- Proficient in accounting software and Microsoft Excel

- Exceptional attention to detail and organizational skills

- Ability to analyze and resolve complex discrepancies

- Excellent time management and ability to prioritize multiple responsibilities

- Strong communication and interpersonal skills

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working in a remote or distributed team environment

- Familiarity with large ERP/accounting systems

- Post-secondary education in Accounting, Finance, or a related field
