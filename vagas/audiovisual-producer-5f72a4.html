Role description (from micro1)

Role Title: Audiovisual Producer

Role Type: Contractor

Location: Remote

Job Summary

micro1 is engaging Audiovisual Producers to contribute their expertise to an innovative AI project. In this role, you will review and annotate robotic video and audiovisual content, assess visual and audio quality, and provide accurate labels and feedback according to project guidelines. Your work will help train next-generation AI systems. No prior experience in AI or data annotation is required — your audiovisual expertise and attention to detail are what matter.

Key Responsibilities

Review and annotate robotic videos and other audiovisual content according to provided guidelines.

Identify important visual and audio elements, events, actions, and quality issues within video content.

Accurately classify and label video segments using predefined annotation categories.

Assess basic video and audio quality, including clarity, synchronization, continuity, framing, and sound.

Identify inconsistencies, errors, or unusual events within audiovisual content.

Provide concise written descriptions and feedback about reviewed content.

Follow annotation instructions consistently and maintain high accuracy across assigned tasks.

Collaborate remotely with project teams and incorporate feedback when needed.

Required Skills and Qualifications

Professional or academic experience in audiovisual production, video production, filmmaking, media production, or a related field.

Good understanding of video and audio fundamentals, including framing, continuity, synchronization, and media quality.

Ability to carefully observe and describe actions and events occurring in video content.

Familiarity with common video editing or audiovisual production tools.

Strong attention to detail and ability to follow structured guidelines.

Good written and verbal English communication skills.

Comfortable reviewing and annotating video content for extended periods.

Ability to work independently in a remote environment.
