Disclosure: the "Apply" buttons are my referral links. If you apply through them, get selected and work 10 hours, micro1 pays me a bonus. It costs you nothing. I don't work for micro1 and I'm not a recruiter: selection, pay and hours are up to them, and nothing is guaranteed. You can also apply directly on micro1.ai.

How it works

  1. Pick a role that fits your background. Check the "Location" field.
  2. Apply on micro1's site and take an AI interview in English (about 20 to 40 minutes). Read the interview guide first.
  3. micro1 decides who gets in. Accepted experts work as independent contractors, remote, flexible hours.

Applying is free. Nobody should ever ask you for money or bank details to start. If they do, it's a scam.
